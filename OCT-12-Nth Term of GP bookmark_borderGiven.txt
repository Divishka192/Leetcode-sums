Nth Term of GP bookmark_borderGiven the first term a, common ratio r, and an integer N, your task is to find Nth term of Geometric Progression(GP).

Input Format
The first and only line of input contains a, r and N.

Output Format
Print Nth term of the Geometric Progression(GP) series.

Constraints
1 <= a <= 20
1 <= r <= 10
1 <= N <= 9

Example
Input
1 2 3 

Output
4

Explanation

Self Explanatory
