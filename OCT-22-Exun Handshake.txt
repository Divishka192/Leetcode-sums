3. Understand the first sample
Input:
N = 4, M = 10
A = [3, 1, 4, 2]


This means:
- There are 4 groups.
- The maximum allowed handshakes are 10.
- The groups contain 3, 1, 4 and 2 people, respectively.
We want to invite the maximum number of groups without exceeding 10 handshakes.

First, sort the group sizes:

[1, 2, 3, 4]

Now check what happens when we invite the smallest groups first.
Groups invited	Total people	Handshakes	Can invite?
Group of 1	1	0	Yes
Groups of 1 and 2	3	3	Yes
Groups of 1, 2 and 3	6	15	No
When we invite 3 groups, there are 6 people, resulting in 15 handshakes. But our limit is only 10.

Therefore, we can invite only 2 groups.

Output: 2
